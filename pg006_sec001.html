Preface

This textbook, Geography for Secondary Schools, is written specifically for Form One students in the United Republic of Tanzania. The book is prepared in accordance with the 2023 Geography Syllabus for Ordinary Secondary Education, Form I–IV, issued by the Ministry of Education Science and Technology (MoEST). It is a revised edition of Geography for Secondary Schools Student’s Book for Form One that was published in 2019 in accordance with the 2005 syllabus issued by the then Ministry of Education and Vocational Training (MoEVT).

The book consists of four (4) chapters, namely the Introduction to Geography, the Planet Earth, Movements of the Earth in the solar system, and Major features of the Earth’s surface. In addition to the contents, each chapter contains activities, scenarios, illustrations, projects, and exercises. You are encouraged to do all the activities, projects, tasks, and questions. As well as other assignments provided by your teacher, your also required to prepare a portfolio for keeping records of activities performed then in different lessons. Doing so will enable you to develop the intended competencies

Additional learning resources are available at TIE e-Library https://ol.tie.go.tz or ol.tie.go.tz
