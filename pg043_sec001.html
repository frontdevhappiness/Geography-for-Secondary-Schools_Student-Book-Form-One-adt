(iii) Since Mtwara is found in the East of Tunis, Mtwara’s time will be ahead of that of Tunis by 2 hours (03.00 pm + 2 hours = 05:00 pm). Therefore, the time in Mtwara will be 05.00 pm when the local time at Tunis 10° E is 03:00 pm.

The longitudes of places are calculated using their local time. Consider the following examples.
