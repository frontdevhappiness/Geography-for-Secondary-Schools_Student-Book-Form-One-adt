Glossary

Altitude
Height of a point above the mean sea level measured vertically
Anemometer
Instrument for measuring wind speed
Aphelion
Point in the orbit of a planet, asteroid, or comet which is the furthest from the sun
Atmosphere
Layer of gases surrounding the earth or another planet
Atmospheric pressure
Pressure exerted by the weight of the atmosphere, which at sea level, has a mean value of 760 mmHg
Autumn
Season of the year, which comes after summer, when leaves fall from trees. In the northern hemisphere it occurs from September to November, and in the southern hemisphere from March to May
Axis
Imaginary line about which a body rotates
Cartography
Science or practice of drawing maps
Centrifugal force
Force that directs a body away from the center around which the body is moving
Centripetal force
Force that directs a body towards the center around which the body is moving
Circumnavigation
Sailing all the way around the world
Environment
Surroundings or conditions in which living organisms operate
Equator
Zero (0°) latitude, dividing the earth into two equal halves
Equinox
Day and night of equal length
Galaxy
Massive collection of stars and interstellar matter isolated in space which are held together by gravitational attraction
Gravitational force
Force that pulls heavenly bodies towards each other or towards the source body
Great circle
Circle on the surface of a sphere that lies in a plane passing through the sphere’s centre
