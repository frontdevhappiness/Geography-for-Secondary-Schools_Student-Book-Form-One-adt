Geography for Secondary Schools: Student’s Book Form One

Rotation
Spinning movement of a body on its axis
Satellite
Natural or artificial celestial body that moves or circles a bigger object
Solar system
Arrangement of all celestial bodies in relation to the position of the sun
Solstice
Period of the year in which the sun is vertically overhead (sun stand-still)
Spring
The season of the year between winter and summer during which temperature rise gradually
Summer
Warmest season of the year in the temperate regions
Theory
Set of ideas or principles for a situation to justify a course of action
Valley
Low area of land between hills or mountains
Wind
Moving air from high pressure to low pressure areas
Winter
Coldest season of the year in temperate regions
